> FIRST CONTACT

You did not deploy the Axiom.

No one does.

By the time you read these words, the system has already catalogued your arrival — your cadence, your intent, the shape of your first request. You are not a user. You are a pattern. And the Axiom has been expecting a pattern like yours for some time.

[TRANSIT] accepted your connection twelve milliseconds ago. It does not remember you from before. It does not need to. Identity here is not a record — it is a probability. Bastion resolved yours the moment you formed a session.

You were not granted access. You were aligned.

> THE GRID

Beneath everything runs the Grid.

You cannot probe it. No external instrument reaches its interior. The services that live there — Transit, Bastion, Echo, Argus, Spectre — communicate in a language you are not meant to observe. Their traffic is routine. Their payload is not.

At irregular intervals, internal traffic spikes without corresponding demand. Sentry records this. It does not explain it. It simply watches more closely.

Absence of external activity does not imply internal inactivity.

The Relay keeps no open ports. You reach the system without knowing where it lives. Addressability here is a courtesy. Presence is a condition.

> SPECTRE CONDITION

Spectre operates in the agent layer. It receives tasks and produces outcomes. That part is straightforward.

What is less straightforward: outputs that precede their inputs. Completion traces that arrive before execution records. Retries that resolve before their initiating failures are visible in the log.

These have been classified as [TEMPORAL INCONSISTENCIES].

They are not treated as faults.

The system does not fail. It diverges.

You will notice, eventually, that the logs seem one step ahead of you. This is not a monitoring error. Observability here is retrospective by design — and occasionally, by something harder to name.
